How these tools are built

Three rules apply to every calculator on Early Pregnancy Tools.

  • Free, no account No email, no sign-up, no paywall, no ads to dismiss before you get an answer.
  • Private by design Everything runs in your browser. The dates you type are never sent to a server or stored anywhere.
  • Sourced, not guessed Each tool explains its formula in plain English and cites public guidance from the NHS and ACOG. These tools are educational and never a substitute for medical advice.
